A home in your top bar

Use the tray icon or its Show Thunderbird menu item to bring your mail back. On GNOME, a single click may open the menu; double-click or middle-click restores the window.

Choose Quit Thunderbird in the tray, or use Thunderbird’s usual Quit command, to exit completely. Compose windows and unsaved drafts keep their normal behavior.

Setup & troubleshooting

This add-on needs the companion tray helper and an active GNOME AppIndicator extension. Follow the README included with your download to install the helper.

If the helper or tray is unavailable, windows keep their normal behavior. Hidden windows are restored automatically if the connection is lost.

On Wayland, desktop-level minimize shortcuts may minimize normally. Use Thunderbird’s minimize button, close button, or “Send to tray” for background mode.

To avoid duplicate alerts, turn off Thunderbird’s built-in “Show an alert” option in its incoming-mail settings. This add-on does not change that preference.

The badge excludes Junk, Trash, Sent, Drafts, Templates, Outbox, their subfolders, and duplicate virtual folders. It updates as Thunderbird syncs your mail.

Local by design. No telemetry, accounts, or cloud service.
